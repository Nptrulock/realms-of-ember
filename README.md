# 🔥 Realms of Ember

A deep incremental idle RPG in an original high-fantasy setting. Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step.

**Play:** https://nptrulock.github.io/realms-of-ember/

- 7 classes with their own resource mechanics (Rage, Mana + Spell Slots, Energy + Combo, Faith, Focus + Wolf, Holy Power, Mana + Souls + Life Tap), skill trees, subclasses, multiclassing
- 9 zones of animated auto-combat with elites, bosses, loot tables, and an Endless Rift
- 6 professions with distinct mechanics (lode streaks, real-time garden plots, forge heat vs. crack risk, recipe mastery, +15 enchanting with gem wards, cooking signatures)
- Parallel action slots (apprentices and familiars), gear rarities, affixes, legendary powers
- 15 ultra-rare relics: account-wide, survive ascension, with drop odds shown in the Codex
- A per-hit damage cap that Limit Breaks raise
- Town QoL unlocks, guild halls, Ascension with astral upgrades, 42 feats, offline progress, autosave, and save export/import

`sim.js` is a headless balance simulator: `node sim.js <class> <hours>`.
